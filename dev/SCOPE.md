# Math AA HL scope (official guide, first assessment 2021; list is exhaustive)

Out of scope examples: Poisson, hypothesis testing, chi-squared, Spearman, confidence intervals, Markov chains,
matrices (except as a method for systems), second-order ODEs, Taylor series about points other than 0.
Paper 1 = no calculator: exact answers, every step by hand. Papers 2/3 = GDC (TI-Nspire CX II non-CAS).

TOPIC 1 NUMBER AND ALGEBRA
- SL: standard form; arithmetic/geometric sequences and series, sigma notation; financial applications
  (compound interest, depreciation); exponent and log laws, change of base; simple deductive proof;
  sum to infinity |r|<1; binomial expansion natural n.
- AHL 1.10 counting principles, nPr, nCr (NOT identical objects, circular); binomial for fractional/negative n.
- AHL 1.11 partial fractions: max two distinct linear factors, numerator degree lower.
- AHL 1.12-1.14 complex numbers: Cartesian, polar, Euler form; conjugate roots; De Moivre powers and roots.
- AHL 1.15 proof by induction, contradiction, counterexample.
- AHL 1.16 systems of up to 3 linear equations in 3 unknowns (unique/none/infinite, general solution).

TOPIC 2 FUNCTIONS
- SL: lines; domain, range, composite, inverse; graphing; quadratics (3 forms, discriminant); reciprocal and
  simple rational functions; exponential and log functions; solving equations analytically/graphically;
  transformations.
- AHL 2.12 factor and remainder theorems, sum and product of roots. 2.13 rational functions
  (ax+b)/(cx^2+dx+e) and (ax^2+bx+c)/(dx+e) incl. oblique asymptotes. 2.14 odd/even, restricted-domain
  inverse, self-inverse. 2.15 inequalities g(x) >= f(x) (analytic for polynomials up to degree 3).
  2.16 |f(x)|, f(|x|), 1/f(x), f(ax+b), [f(x)]^2; modulus equations and inequalities.

TOPIC 3 GEOMETRY AND TRIGONOMETRY
- SL: 3D distance, midpoint, volume, surface area, angles; sine rule incl. ambiguous case, cosine rule,
  1/2 ab sinC; arcs and sectors; unit circle, exact values; Pythagorean and double-angle identities;
  circular function graphs; trig equations in a finite interval (general solutions NOT required).
- AHL 3.9 sec, cosec, cot, Pythagorean identities; arcsin, arccos, arctan. 3.10 compound angles, double angle
  for tan. 3.11 symmetry identities. 3.12-3.18 vectors, scalar/vector product, lines, planes, intersections, angles.

TOPIC 4 STATISTICS AND PROBABILITY
- 4.1-4.4 sampling, outliers (1.5 IQR), frequency tables, quartiles, box plots, mean/median/mode, grouped mean,
  sd/variance (technology), effect of linear transforms, Pearson r and regression (technology).
- 4.5-4.7 probability rules, Venn/tree, conditional, independence, discrete RVs, E(X), fair games.
- 4.8 binomial; 4.9 normal (technology); 4.10 regression x on y; 4.11 formal conditional probability;
  4.12 standardisation, inverse normal with unknown mean or sd. AHL 4.13 Bayes (max 3 events).
  AHL 4.14 Var of discrete RV; continuous RVs/pdfs (incl. piecewise): k, mode, median, E(X), Var(X),
  E(aX+b), Var(aX+b).

TOPIC 5 CALCULUS
- 5.1-5.11: limits idea; increasing/decreasing; derivatives of x^n, sin, cos, e^x, ln x; chain/product/quotient;
  tangents and normals; antiderivatives with boundary condition; definite integrals; second derivative;
  max/min, optimisation, inflexion (f''=0 AND sign change), concavity; kinematics (distance = int |v|);
  integrals of x^n, sin, cos, 1/x, e^x and linear composites; reverse chain / substitution for k g'(x)f(g(x));
  areas (f positive or negative) and between curves.
- AHL 5.12 continuity/differentiability (informal), first principles for polynomials only, higher derivatives.
- AHL 5.13 limits 0/0 and inf/inf by l'Hopital (repeated) or Maclaurin series.
- AHL 5.14 implicit differentiation, related rates, optimisation incl. end points.
- AHL 5.15 derivatives of tan, sec, cosec, cot, a^x, log_a x, arcsin, arccos, arctan; their integrals and linear
  composites; partial fractions to rearrange integrands.
- AHL 5.16 substitution (examiners give u unless form k g'(x) f(g(x))); integration by parts incl. repeated.
- AHL 5.17 area between curve and y-axis; volumes of revolution about x- or y-axis.
- AHL 5.18 first-order ODEs: Euler's method, separation of variables, homogeneous y = vx, integrating factor.
- AHL 5.19 Maclaurin series of e^x, sin x, cos x, arctan x, ln(1+x), (1+x)^p; by substitution, products,
  integration, differentiation; series from differential equations.
